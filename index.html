Not a test. A test drive.

CIPAT lets you experience a major from the inside before you commit to it. There are no right or wrong answers. The only thing that counts is how it feels to you.

Experience it

Walk through the world of a major and watch real moments from its classrooms, labs, and workplaces.

Say how it feels

Get your fit report

AI turns your answers into a clear report, and suggests other majors if this one is not quite you.

Your journey, level by level

  1. Pick a majorBrowse the list, or tell the AI what you love and let it suggest a few.
  2. Explore its worldWalk in, watch five moments, ask a senior, then decide whether to go on.
  3. Take three levelsBasic, Medium, and Deep show how far into the major you go, not how hard it is. Each class has a lesson, a task, and a quick feeling check.
  4. Read your fit reportSee what you enjoyed, what bothered you, and how it connects to real careers.
